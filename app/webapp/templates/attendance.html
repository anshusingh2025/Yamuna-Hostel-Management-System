📋 Attendance Description

Hostel attendance is taken every day at 6:00 PM. There are a total of 20 rooms in the hostel. Each room accommodates either 3 or 4 students. The hostel staff records the attendance of every student by selecting Present or Absent for the selected date. The attendance record helps the hostel administration monitor student presence and maintain proper hostel discipline and safety.
